; units set (docs/ART.md 10). Bespoke sprites are named unit/<type without UNIT_> (EQUIPMENT_X:
; unit/equipment_x) and need no rule. Until one exists, a unit resolves to the default unit of
; its class (unique units), then to the category rules below (most conditions wins; prio adds).
; Exact rules (naming a type) come first: they give faith accents, material remaps and ghosts, and
; stop a unique unit of another nature from borrowing its base unit's look (an undead axeman is
; not an axeman).
;
; Sources: generic.spr (category silhouettes, equipment glyphs), infantry.spr, mounted.spr,
; robed.spr (arcane, divine, civilians, great people), siege_naval.spr, animals.spr (and beasts),
; natures.spr (demons, undead, angels, golems, elementals), heroes.spr.
; Stack presentation: units/stack.js (loadArt installs it, art.js PASSES; or drawStackOverlay)
; draws the standard, count, health, moves and the lantern reflections on water; units/preview.html shows it,
; every tincture in every state, the naval line and the weapon lines, a muster and the roster.
;   standard  ui/stack_banner (ui/heraldry.spr), 7x12: pole, crossbar, a 5x8 field, 5x5 sigil;
;             top-left at tile (1,1), mirrored to the top-right in cities. Quiet by default (the
;             tincture's shade as the field); the full tincture only on your own movable or
;             selected stack; the vellum finial pip says 'can move'. Designed here, adopted
;             verbatim by the ui set (ui/heraldry.spr); stack.js reads its geometry from
;             `: flag` and `: finial`.
;   lanterns  ship and settler lanterns are a glow pixel over an amber one in a void frame,
;             FIRE radius 6; on water stack.js adds the ART.md 5.4 reflection under each light
;             (E E e e dashes, 2 px apart, from the waterline). A ship whose oars or hull dip
;             below its waterline names it: `: tags waterline=<row>`.
;   naval     one silhouette feature per line ship at 1x: galleon two masts and a stern castle,
;             frigate three narrow sails and one gun-port row, man-o-war two rows, queen of the
;             line three gun decks and a long owner pennant from the main truck.
; reference.png (stacks, standards, naval line at 2x, the muster) and reference-roster.png are
; small reference sheets of preview.html (regenerate them from its screenshots when the set
; changes).
