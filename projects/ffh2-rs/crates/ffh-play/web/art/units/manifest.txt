; units set (docs/ART.md 10). Bespoke sprites are named unit/<type without UNIT_> (EQUIPMENT_X:
; unit/equipment_x) and need no rule. Until one exists, a unit resolves to the default unit of
; its class (unique units), then to the category rules below (most conditions wins; prio adds).
; Exact rules (naming a type) come first: they give faith accents, material remaps and ghosts, and
; stop a unique unit of another nature from borrowing its base unit's look (an undead axeman is
; not an axeman).
;
; Sources: atlas.spr (figures cut from the unit atlases, tools/atlas/quantize.py) and composed.spr
; (figures composed from atlas parts and painted shapes, tools/atlas/compose_units.py).
; Stack presentation: units/stack.js (loadArt installs it, art.js PASSES; or drawStackOverlay)
; draws the stack marks in the corners and on the top edge (count chip, hero pip, health bar, state
; mark), the quiet ring of your spent stacks and the lantern reflections on water; units/preview.html
; shows it, every tincture in every state, the naval line and the weapon lines, a muster and the roster.
;   ring      the team ring under the feet (art.js) carries ownership; stack.js passes a quiet ring
;             (the tincture's shade as the field) for your own spent stack: a bright ring says
;             'can move'.
;   standard  ui/stack_banner (ui/heraldry.spr), the 7x12 gonfalon of the night look, is opt-in
;             (state.standard 'left' | 'right'); stack.js reads its geometry from `: flag`.
;   lanterns  ship and settler lanterns are a glow pixel over an amber one in a void frame,
;             FIRE radius 6; on water stack.js adds the ART.md 5.4 reflection under each light
;             (E E e e dashes, 2 px apart, from the waterline). A ship whose oars or hull dip
;             below its waterline names it: `: tags waterline=<row>`.
; reference.png (stacks, standards, naval line at 2x, the muster) and reference-roster.png are
; small reference sheets of preview.html (regenerate them from its screenshots when the set
; changes).
