; terrain set: ground textures, hills, peaks, features, landmarks (unique features), routes
; (docs/ART.md 5, 6.2, 8.4). Rule syntax: map <kind> <conditions> -> <sprite|none> [options]
;
; Minimap colours: every terrain, hill, peak and feature rule carries `mini=K`, the palette key of
; one minimap pixel (docs/ART.md 8.4). A tile's colour is its feature's `mini` if the feature has
; one, else its peak's or hill's, else its terrain's; owned land takes the owner's field colour,
; fogged tiles pass through FOG, cities are vellum `8`, unrevealed tiles void `0`. Read it with
; art.resolve(kind, type).opts.mini.
;
; Notes on the set (against docs/ART.md 5): ground mottling is drawn as sparse 1-3 px flecks in
; noise-shaped regions rather than 25%/50% lattices (the lattices read as perforation at 2x);
; desert is ash `5` with sand flecks and dune arcs so large deserts stay inside the value budget;
; snow is wind streaks of frost `f` over slate `4` and glint `g` (about half, a third, a sixth),
; with rime `F` on rare crests only: a cold grey that stays under the light of cities (round 3: half
; glint read as a vivid steel-blue field); remembered (REMEMBER in ground.js) it is slate and coal;
; burning sands is umber `u` with rust `x` clumps under ember dune crests (the fire comes from
; FEATURE_FLAMES, not the ground); hills leave their lit body open and close their dark side with a
; checkered flank and a void contour; peaks are massifs of two or three peaks whose outline stops
; above the foot, which dissolves into the ground, so ranges merge instead of stacking in rows;
; jungle is drooping fronds and hanging vines over a void underside, not round clumps; the unique
; features (landmarks) are drawn here and claim their IMPROVEMENT_* types; the sacred sites carry
; RITE pools of 14 to 24 px (the light pass draws them as a teal haze with a ritual heart), and the
; standing stones are five menhirs round a glowing pale-green ellipse, the board's ritual circle,
; with one wide pool; Carcer keeps its wide BLOOD pool, an inner pass and three small pools.
;
; ground.js (installTerrain) is the set's ground pass over art.js's seed drawing: a pixel takes
; the terrain class with the largest sum of Gaussian weights (sigma 13 px, each tile's weight scaled
; by a positional hash) of the 3x3 tile centres, sampled through a three-octave warp (cells 46/22/7
; px, |w| <= 14), so single tiles become blobs and runs of edges wander; 4 px Bayer transition bands
; between land textures; moonlit clearings, cold grain and cloud shade from a low-frequency field;
; the fog and unrevealed edges classified the same way (full warp, 4 and 3 px bands) and remembered
; through REMEMBER (grey snow); pale paths that sway, with a worn verge; river verges (a 50% checker
; one step lighter); hills never mirrored; wooded hills with trees on the crests and the mounds kept
; clear; a 1 px moonlit lichen rim on the north-west edge of every wood mass. loadArt() installs it
; (art.js PASSES); loadArt({ passes: false }) gives the seed drawing. terrain/preview.html: plates for
; the set (?map=sample|mosaic|north|relief|woods|landmarks, ?pass=0 for the seed ground, ?light=0
; without the light pass); ref/: the shared scene through ground.js at 2x (with the review sets) and
; the relief plate at 3x (hills, wooded hills, flat woods, peaks on every land terrain).
set terrain

source relief.spr
