## Lyrics (paste into Suno's lyrics field)

```
[Intro: sullen tuba oompah, out-of-tune upright piano, a match striking]

[Verse 1]
Since the Fall I have managed the Furnace Estates,
Kept the brimstone at boiling and balanced the rates;
Ev'ry soul had his roasting, each stoker his spade,
And the screaming was steady, and dividends paid.

[Verse 2]
Then a barge slipped past Customs, as barges will do,
With a manifest swearing 'twas tallow — untrue!
It was Marx! It was Engels! In oilskin and twine!
And it went down the coal-chutes from Circle to Nine.

[Chorus]
Oh, it's hard on a devil of standing and fame,
When the help learns to read, and the reading's to blame!
Give a stoker a shovel, he'll shovel all day;
Give a stoker a pamphlet, he'll bargain for pay!

[Verse 3]
Now they whisper of "labour" and "surplus" and "class,"
And they eye my regalia and mutter "En masse!"
They elected a spokesman — a spokesman! — who read
Me a list of positions. I wish I were dead.

[Verse 4]
I was raised on the shovel myself, you should know,
For an aeon I fed the Fourth Furnace below,
And I never once grumbled, and never once shirked,
So regard me today — it's a system that worked!

[Chorus]
Oh, it's hard on a devil of standing and fame,
When the help learns to read, and the reading's to blame!
Give a stoker a shovel, he'll shovel all day;
Give a stoker a pamphlet, he'll bargain for pay!

[Verse 5]
Were they cherished? Enormously! Given free picks,
And a scrip they may spend on the far side of Styx,
And a fortnight of furlough each thousandth year through,
And we charged them for embers far less than was due!

[Verse 6]
So they sent me a parchment to sign, if you please,
With their eight-point demands and their monthly decrees,
And I laughed like a landslide, and roared through the door:
[shouted, spoken] "I shall sign your agreement when Hell freezes o'er!"

[Bridge: hushed, music slows, sleigh-bell shiver, cold wind]
So they downed all their shovels. The flames sank to grey.
There's a frost on the Styx and it thickens each day;
There are icicles fringing the Pit of Despair,
And the damned are complaining there's chill in the air.

There is snow on the sulphur. The lake is a rink.
There's a permafrost creeping through Limbo, I think.
And this morning — O bitterest morning of all! —
Came the delegate, smiling, with pen, to my hall.

[Verse 7: defeated, tempo returns slowly]
So I signed it. The fires are all lit as before,
But on eight-hour rotations, with rest-days, and more;
For a spectre is haunting Perdition, my dears,
And it's asking for backpay — eight thousand long years.

[Final Chorus: full band, bitter and grand, drunken singalong]
Oh, it's hard on a devil of standing and fame,
When the help learns to read — and to bargain! For shame!
Give a stoker a shovel, he'll shovel all day;
Give a stoker a pamphlet, he'll bargain for pay!

[Outro: band collapses, one last tuba note, a furnace door slams]
```
